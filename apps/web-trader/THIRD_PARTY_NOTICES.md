# GlobeMorph visual

The requested Framer GlobeMorph asset delegates to the external renderer at https://tkartik.com/globe-to-flat-map/van-der-grinten-map.html. The CTA uses that renderer with theme-specific appearance parameters. Its local reduced-motion fallback uses orthographic outlines derived from the renderer's public-domain Natural Earth data. Natural Earth terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.
